THE PROCESS

Clear steps. No confusion.

From the first message to the finished project, here's how we can work together.

01

Share your idea

Pick a package and explain what you want to create. Tell me who the project is for, what it should do, and what style you like.

02

Confirm the scope

We'll clarify the pages, content, features, and what is included in the price. Custom projects receive a quote after the requirements are understood.

03

Provide your content

Send the text, images, logo, links, and other materials you want included. Only share files you have permission to use.

04

Build and review

The design is prepared according to the agreed scope. Check the details and share any feedback covered by the package.

05

Receive your project

You'll receive the agreed files or delivery details. Hosting, domains, and publishing are discussed separately if needed.
